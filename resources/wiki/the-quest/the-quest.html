Tutorial: a lighthouse app for phones

In five lessons you build an Android app in Go that looks and behaves like Android’s own: a list of lighthouses, a screen for each, a dialog before removing one, a note to undo it, settings for the theme and the colours, and the back gesture. You try it on the desktop as you go, and end with it installed on a phone.

Before you start, know go-ui’s basics: its Get started and, better, the first lessons of its tutorial. This tutorial uses the same immediate mode, with Material pieces instead of go-ui’s own.

Every lesson has the same shape, as in a quest: the idea in everyday words, the code, a checkpoint to know it works, a bonus with its solution folded, and an achievement to unlock. A final challenge waits at the end of lesson 5.

Lesson You learn You end with
1. A first screen material.Look, schemes, the top bar, list items, the floating button The list, in a phone-sized window
2. Screens and the back gesture The navigation bar, a stack of screens, GoBack, Back A screen for each lighthouse
3. Fields, dialogs and notes Text fields, sliders, switches, Dialog, Snackbar Editing, removing and undoing
4. Colours and settings The wallpaper’s colours, a scheme from one colour, the dark theme A settings screen
5. On a phone The SDK, the NDK, go-ui-apk, the emulator, the log, a release The app installed

The whole program is at the end of lesson 4.
