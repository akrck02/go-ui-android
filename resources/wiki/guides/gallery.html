🖼️ The gallery

cmd/gallery has a screen for each part of the catalogue, one file each (-screen 0 to 4 opens on one, for captures):

ActionsInputsListsFeedbackTabs

It was tried on a Pixel 6 Pro (Android 16, the wallpaper’s colours, dark), the Android 16 emulator (light, turned all the way round) and a OnePlus 7 Pro (Android 10, Material’s baseline): every screen, the gestures, the keyboard, a notification, going to the background and back. Its test (cmd/gallery) touches every destination from every other with a finger; the material package’s tests drive each piece with a fake pointer and fake fingers, with no display.
